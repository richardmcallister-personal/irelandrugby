Good to know

  • Every match for the teams you pick: Six Nations, Nations Championship, URC, Champions Cup and Challenge Cup. Each one shows as a two-hour event with the venue and competition.
  • Finished matches stay in for four months with the score in the title.
  • The calendar updates every few hours. Phones check it on their own schedule: Apple devices usually within a few hours, Google Calendar can take up to a day.
  • Provincial fixtures not yet in our results feed are copied from the teams' own lists, so their times can still move. When they do, the event moves rather than doubling up.
  • To stop it, delete the subscribed calendar in your calendar app. Nothing is stored about you; the link only names the teams.
